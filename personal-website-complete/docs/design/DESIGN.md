## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an intimate, editorial, and tactile visual identity for personal portfolios and creative practitioners. It harmonizes high-end editorial refinement with warm, approachable organic tonalities. The aesthetic draws upon:

- **Soft Editorial & Tactile Modernism:** High-contrast, expressive display typography alongside clean, disciplined monoline sans-serifs.
- **Diffuse Glassmorphism & Chromatic Blur:** Luminous, organic gradients reminiscent of dusk light—flowing between warm melon/peach, creamy terracotta, and muted sage greens. Surfaces sit within this soft chromatic field using translucent layered glass, gentle ambient shadows, and fine hairlines.
- **Sculptural Geometry:** Pill-shaped tactile containers, envelope-style layered geometric media holders, and floating pill-shaped navigation triggers.

The user interface feels inviting, calm, poetic, and art-directed, avoiding clinical tech tropes in favor of warmth and deliberate typography.

## Colors

The palette is built around warm, radiant organic tones anchored by grounding herbal greens:

- **Primary (`#FF7865` / `rgb(255, 120, 101)`):** Vibrant salmon-coral used for key focal accents, the brand badge, and primary tactile pills.
- **Secondary (`#4E8057` / `rgb(78, 128, 87)`):** Muted forest/sage green used for secondary CTA buttons, decorative accent cards, active indicators, and playback controls.
- **Tertiary (`#FBA28C` / `rgb(251, 162, 140)`):** Soft melon tone facilitating smooth chromatic gradations across surface backdrops.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#FCE3D8` to `#E5DDD1`):** A warm diffused radial/linear mesh blending from creamy peach-coral into soft sage-tinted olive mist.
- **Text & Details:** High-key white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated display headlines and frosted pill text; rich dark warm espresso (`#2A1D1A`) for ultra-small body and functional instructional copy; muted terracotta-coral (`#E87865`) for subtle text links and rule-lines.

## Typography

The typography strategy balances classical editorial elegance with monoline typographic precision:

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries romantic, expressive serifs with crisp contrast. Display titles use all-caps treatment with custom delicate ligatures or flared terminal detailing to anchor the hero composition.
- **Labels, Navigation & Body (`Manrope`):** Clean, geometric grotesque sans-serif. Navigation elements and category markers employ tracked uppercase settings (`letter-spacing: 0.14em` - `0.18em`) for balance against the warm background.
- **Body & Captions:** Kept concise, high-contrast, and small to maintain spaciousness and generous negative space throughout the presentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a spacious, edge-framed canvas with an editorial stage concept:

- **Outer Enclosure:** A generous outer margin (`3.5rem` desktop, `1.25rem` mobile) frames the entire screen with a neutral ambient perimeter, acting like an art gallery matboard.
- **Hero Asymmetry:** Split dynamic layout with a left-aligned headline block and right-shifted sculptural media centerpiece.
- **Horizontal Anchoring & Rail Lines:** Slender 1px structural guidelines (`#E48C7B` / `#708A72`) connect navigation pills to links and vertical track selectors, maintaining horizontal alignment across differing content weights.
- **Vertical Navigation Rail:** Positioned along the right margin, featuring up/down chevron indicators and a vertical state indicator bar for section jumps.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** Fixed-height stage view with split layout and active vertical selector rail.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Compacted navigation links, reduced hero headline scale (`headline-lg`), centered media card.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column flow; navigation folds into a top-right overlay button; media stack tucks beneath headline; bottom utility links float as pinned translucent chips.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ambient luminescence, glass backdrops, and physical surface overlaps rather than drop shadows:

- **Luminous Tinted Glows:** Interactive pill buttons project diffused, warm-tinted light blooms (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(255, 120, 101, 0.45)` for primary coral, `0 10px 30px rgba(78, 128, 87, 0.35)` for sage buttons).
- **Frosted Glass Containers:** Semi-translucent panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)`) with subtle backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) and fine inner borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`).
- **Paper Stack Metaphor:** Interactive portfolio previews layer rotated card rectangles (rotated between -12° and +10°) inside an envelope-style housing, creating physical tactile depth through overlap and angular tension.

## Shapes

The design system uses a pill-forward shape hierarchy:

- **Full Stadium / Capsule (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Used for identity badges ("PARDOFELIS"), language toggles ("ENG"), interactive CTA buttons ("CONTACT"), and the circular media play button.
- **Strict Geometric Corners (`rounded-none` / `0px`):** Used on inner showcase cards, accent squares, and media polaroids to establish contrast against the fluid, curved pill elements.
- **Framed Envelope Housing (`rounded-sm` / `4px` - `8px`):** Minimal rounding applied to the primary showcase media vessel.

## Components

### 1. Pill Badges & Buttons
- **Primary Coral Badge / Button:** Background `#FF7865`, full capsule radius, white uppercase tracking text (`Manrope`, 11px, weight 700). Ambient coral drop glow (`0 8px 24px rgba(255, 120, 101, 0.4)`).
- **Accent Green CTA Button:** Background `#4E8057`, full capsule radius, white uppercase tracking text, ambient moss glow.
- **Language / Secondary Filter Pill:** Ghost pill with translucent warm border (`rgba(255, 120, 101, 0.4)`), soft background tint (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), and dark salmon text.

### 2. Header & Nav Rail
- **Header Line-Track:** Minimal horizontal 1px lines extending between badge anchors and text links, colored to match their regional context (coral in the warm gradient zone, sage green in the cooler top-right quadrant).
- **Nav Links:** Uppercase text links without underlines. Active state is highlighted in bold coral (`#FF7865`); inactive links in dark espresso (`#382926`) with 70% opacity.

### 3. Media Portfolio Showcase ("Envelope Stack")
- **Base Container:** Coral-tinted frame (`#FBA28C`) acting as a folder/frame.
- **Layered Elements:** Overlapping rectangular preview cards in stark white, deep forest green (`#2F6E3B`), and vibrant salmon (`#FF7865`), tilted diagonally.
- **Action Control:** Floating circular play button (`#FFFFFF` fill, 64px diameter) centered at the bottom fold, with a green stroked open-triangle icon (`#4E8057`).

### 4. Right-Hand Rail Selector
- **Stack:** Vertical column containing navigation chevrons (up/down outlines), uppercase destination titles ("MY HOBBIES", "PREREQUISITE", "MY DREAMS"), and a fine vertical tracker line.
- **Typography:** Muted coral/brown, tracking 0.16em, font size 12px, highlighting active slide states with weight and color saturation changes.
